WEATHERED

Weathered describes the condition of an object or material that has gradually changed in appearance or texture through prolonged exposure to natural elements, regular use, or the passage of time. It often refers to surfaces that have become faded, worn, roughened, or altered through contact with factors such as sunlight, wind, rain, and temperature changes. A weathered object may show visible signs of ageing, including changes in colour, texture, and finish, reflecting its exposure to the surrounding environment. This condition does not necessarily suggest that something is damaged or poorly maintained, but rather that it has naturally evolved over time. Weathering can give an object a more aged, softened, and individual appearance, revealing the effects of time and environmental exposure.
