PATINA

Patina describes the subtle surface changes that develop on an object over time as a result of age, use, exposure, and natural wear.

It often appears as a change in colour, texture, or finish, giving a material a more aged and distinctive appearance.

Patina is commonly associated with materials such as leather, wood, metal, and stone, where gradual changes in the surface reflect the

object's history and interaction with its surroundings. Rather than simply indicating damage or deterioration, patina can be seen as a natural

characteristic that adds depth, character, and individuality to an object. It represents the visual evidence of time and use, allowing a material to develop an appearance that cannot be fully replicated when new.
