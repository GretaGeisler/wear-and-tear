DEFECTIVE

Defective describes the condition of an object, material, or product that has a fault, flaw, or imperfection affecting its intended quality, appearance, or functionality.
It refers to something that does not fully meet its expected standards or perform as it was originally designed to. A defect may be present
from the moment of production or develop over time through use, damage, or material deterioration.
It can range from minor imperfections, such as irregularities in a surface, uneven finishes, or small flaws, to more significant issues that affect
an object's structural integrity or usability. Overall, the term describes something that deviates from its intended condition due to a specific fault or weakness.
