Decay level of the sub-pages

    Every interaction leaves a trace. Clicks, hover time, mouse distance, scroll distance and key presses are counted in this browser and weighted into a single value: wear. Wear starts at 0 % at t0, the moment of your first visit, and only ever goes up. [Each metric contributes with its own weight: e.g. 1 click = x %, 1,000 px of mouse travel = y %.]

    The sub-pages read the same value. The more wear has accumulated, the more the interface shows it: burn-in on the screen, grey text ghosts, [more noise, blur and distortion]. Nothing is reset when you close the tab. The state is stored locally and continues at your next visit.
