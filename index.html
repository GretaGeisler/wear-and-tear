WEAR AND TEAR

Wear and Tear is a website that ages while you use it.
Every click, every movement of the cursor, every scroll and key press is counted in your browser and adds up to wear.
The more you interact, the more the interface shows it: burn-in, grey ghosts, noise and distortion.
Seven stages, from pristine to defective, show how far it has gone.
Nothing is reset when you close the tab. The traces stay.

  • Pristine
  • Settled
  • Patina
  • Worn
  • Weathered
  • Neglected
  • Defective
